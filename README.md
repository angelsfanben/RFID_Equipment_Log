RFID Equipment Log
ECE 3130 - Microcomputer Systems - Term Project

Devices:
- Nucleo L476RG
- EduBase-V2-F446
- Raspberry Pi Pico WH
- RFID-RCC522

Purpose:
This system was designed and implemented to create an organization scheme for a research lab at Tennessee Tech University. With many graduate and undergraduate students borrowing tools and measuring instruments, it is important to ensure all lab capital is returned. To keep students accountable and increase the organization and productivity of the lab, our team built the RFID Equipment Log.
